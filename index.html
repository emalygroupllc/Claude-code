How it works

From your pocket to their contacts in three seconds

    Build your profile

Questions

Before you tap

Does the other person need an app?

No. Your profile opens in their phone's browser the moment you tap. They can save your contact with one touch — nothing to install on either side.

Which phones does it work with?

Any iPhone since the iPhone 7 and virtually every Android phone made in the last decade reads NFC out of the box. For anything older, the QR code printed on the back of your card does the same job with the camera.

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once for the card and your digital profile is included, with free edits forever. There's nothing to renew.

What happens if I change jobs or numbers?

Log in, edit your profile, done. The card in your pocket instantly shares the new version — it's a key to your profile, not a copy of it.

Does the card need charging?

Never. The copper coil inside is powered by the phone it touches, so the card has no battery and nothing to wear out.

Can I put my company's logo on the cards?

Yes — every card can be printed or engraved with your own design, and team orders include matching branding across all profiles.
